Civic information

Who governs Niwot, and where the official information is

Niwot is an unincorporated community in Boulder County, Colorado. It has no town government: the county, a set of special districts and the school district provide its public services. On November 3, 2026, voters inside a proposed boundary decide whether that should change.

This section is a neutral route to the official bodies. TownofNiwot.com is not one of them and takes no position on incorporation. Last verified September 16, 2026.

Civic status

An unincorporated community

The U.S. Census Bureau counts Niwot as a census designated place, a statistical area with no government of its own; it recorded 4,306 residents there in 2020. Day to day, that means:

Boulder County

The three elected County Commissioners are the local legislative body. County departments handle land use and building permits, roads, the sheriff, parks and open space, public health, property records, and elections through the Clerk and Recorder.

bouldercounty.gov ↗

Special districts

Fire and emergency medical response come from Mountain View Fire Rescue, drinking water from the Left Hand Water District, sewer service from the Niwot Sanitation District where its lines run, and schools from the St. Vrain Valley School District. Each is a separate public body with its own board.

The business district

The Niwot Local Improvement District is a county district funded by a 1% sales tax in the commercial area. An advisory committee recommends how it is spent; the County Commissioners, sitting as its board, decide.

Niwot LID at Boulder County ↗

Community groups such as the Niwot Business Association, the Niwot Community Association and the Niwot Cultural Arts Association are private organizations. They do a great deal here, but none of them is a government or speaks officially for every resident. The community page describes each one and what it runs.

Current election

2026 incorporation election

November 3, 2026

Registered electors living inside the boundary described in the incorporation petition vote on whether Niwot should become a municipality, whether to form a home rule charter commission, who would sit on it, and five fiscal questions that apply only if a town is created. The Niwot Election Commission, appointed by the Boulder County District Court, sets the ballot content and procedure; the Boulder County Clerk and Recorder conducts the coordinated election.

The guide summarizes the ballot in plain language and links each question to the certified text. It does not recommend a vote. After the election it will carry the official status as the authorities publish it, first as preliminary and then as certified results, and stay online as the record.

How this section is kept

Neutral, sourced, dated

Civic pages here rest on filed documents and official publications, name the authority responsible, and carry the date they were last checked. Campaign material is linked only where it is labeled as such. Election pages stay online after the vote with their outcome and an archive label, so the record does not disappear when the question is settled. Anything that cannot be supported is removed rather than softened, and every change to a published fact is logged with its date.
