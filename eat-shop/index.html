How this directory is compiled

A curated directory of Niwot businesses, checked against business-owned and community sources: each business’s own site, the Niwot Business Association directory and the Left Hand Valley Courier. Phone numbers are not reproduced here because they change faster than anything else — each row links out for current hours and contact details, and says where the link goes. A business that keeps no public listing will not appear; send yours and it goes in.
