Contribute

Send a listing, an event or a correction

Business owners, event organizers and residents keep this guide accurate. Send yours with a link to its source and it goes in with the date it was verified.

The form

By sending this you agree that the editor may check it against its source and publish what it supports, with the date it was verified. Sending it does not guarantee publication, and nothing is published without review. Your email is optional and is used only to reply about this submission; sending a correction does not sign you up for anything. How submissions are handled.

What goes in

With its source, and with its date

A business listing needs a name, a category, an address and a website or the Business Association listing. An event needs the organizer’s own page with the date on it. A correction needs the page, what is wrong and what it should say.

Or write directly

editor@townofniwot.com reaches the editor without the form. Use it for anything the form does not fit, and if the form ever fails to send.

Listings and events are published with the date they were checked. A correction to a published fact is listed on Our Story with its date and a note of what changed. A claim that cannot be supported is removed rather than softened.
