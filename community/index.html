Community Groups & Resident Resources

Community groups

Volunteer and membership organizations. Each line says what the group actually runs, so you know which one to approach for what.

  • 01

    Niwot Business Association

    The membership organization for local businesses: the members’ directory with current hours, the community events calendar, and the seasonal gatherings in Old Town.

    Runs: Business directory, events calendar, seasonal gatherings in Old Town
    niwot.com ↗
  • 02

    Niwot Cultural Arts Association

    A volunteer non-profit, formed in 2009, that funds and organizes the arts in Niwot, from Whistle Stop Park and the Sculpture Park to the summer concerts.

    Runs: Whistle Stop Park, Niwot Sculpture Park, Why Not Niwot?, Rock & Rails
    niwotarts.org ↗
  • 03

    Niwot Historical Society

    Preserves and shares the history of Niwot: an online timeline of 150 years, walking tours, lectures and archives.

    Runs: History timeline, walking tours, lectures, archives
    niwothistoricalsociety.org ↗
  • 04

    Niwot Community Association

    An all-volunteer non-profit of household members, established in 1990, that speaks for residents to Boulder County and holds community events.

    Runs: Residents’ voice to the county, community events
    niwot.org ↗

Public bodies with a Niwot remit

Public administration, not clubs: each is part of, or appointed through, government, and is approached as such.

  • Public body

    Niwot Election Commission

    Appointed by the Boulder County District Court to administer the November 2026 incorporation election; it publishes the official ballot language and takes no position on the question.

    Runs: Official ballot language, election notices, voter information
    niwotelection.org ↗
  • Public body

    Niwot Local Improvement District

    A Boulder County improvement district funded by a 1% sales tax in the business area; an advisory committee recommends spending to the County Commissioners, who serve as its board.

    Runs: Sales-tax spending on the business district, decided by the county
    bouldercounty.gov ↗
Where community happens

Two blocks, one park, a lot of volunteers.

The summer concerts, art walks and seasonal gatherings are not municipal programs. They happen because residents and local organizations build the calendar, maintain the public art and keep showing up.

Sewer and trash

Sewer service comes from the Niwot Sanitation District where its lines run — a special district with its own elected board, funded by its service fees, covering about seven square miles around Niwot — and from septic systems elsewhere. Check your address with the district. Trash and recycling collection is arranged household by household with a private hauler; there is no municipal service.
