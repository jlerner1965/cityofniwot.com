NIWOT

A small place worth knowing.

Boulder County, ColoradoPop. 4,306 — 2020 censusWalkable in an afternoon

Two blocks of brick storefronts, a red caboose in the park, and open fields where the sidewalks stop. Park once on Second Avenue for lunch on a patio and the shops and galleries, then follow the trail out toward the Front Range.

Explore Niwot

Start on Second Avenue

Park once. The two commercial blocks are a fifteen-minute walk apart; the park and the trail are at their edges.

Old Town

The brick commercial block on Second Avenue, walkable end to end in about ten minutes.

Cottonwood Square

Parks & Trails

Community Groups & Resident Resources
