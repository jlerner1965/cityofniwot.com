Visit Niwot: Directions, Parking & Accessibility

Niwot sits off the Diagonal Highway between Boulder and Longmont. Leave the car on or near Second Avenue: the two commercial blocks are within a fifteen-minute walk of each other, and the park and the trail are at their edges.

A simple first visit

See the town without moving the car.

Start on Second Avenue, walk through the park and choose either Cottonwood Square or the trail for the last leg. The sequence keeps the day compact without turning it into a rigid itinerary.

On the ground

Parking, walking and transit

Niwot is unincorporated, so parking rules, road work and trail closures are set by Boulder County rather than a town. Posted signs override anything written here. The county pages and districts responsible for each service are listed once, under resident resources.

Trail closures ↗ Route BOLT ↗
Parking
Street parking along Second Avenue and lot parking serving the Old Town block and Cottonwood Square. Free at time of writing; posted restrictions apply.
On foot
Old Town is walkable end to end in about ten minutes, with continuous sidewalks and short crossings.
By bike
The Longmont-to-Boulder (LoBo) Regional Trail runs through Niwot and connects to quiet county roads. The county’s trail page carries the map.
Transit
RTD’s Route BOLT runs between Boulder and Longmont along the Diagonal, with a stop at Highway 119 and Niwot Road. Construction of the CO 119 bus rapid transit line has been changing stops on this corridor, so check the route page before traveling.
Dogs
Leash and waste rules on trails and open space are set by Boulder County Parks & Open Space.
Best time
Weekday mornings for a quiet walk. Summer Thursday evenings for the concert series beside the caboose.
Accessibility

What we can confirm

Second Avenue has continuous sidewalks with curb ramps at the cross streets, and the Whistle Stop Park lawn is reached from a paved path. Entrances vary across the historic block: several have a single step at the door and some have raised wooden porches.

Step-free access differs building by building, so contact a business directly before relying on it. Public restrooms and designated accessible parking have not yet been confirmed on the ground and are not claimed here.
