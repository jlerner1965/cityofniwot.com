Privacy

What this site keeps, and what it does not

This guide collects the information you choose to submit through its one form, the submission form on the contact page. The hosting and font providers also process the limited technical information needed to deliver the site. This page explains what is collected, why, how long it is kept, and how to reach the editor.

Effective September 9, 2026 · Last updated September 14, 2026

Who runs this guide

TownofNiwot.com is an independent community guide to Niwot, Colorado, published and edited privately by a resident. It is not a municipal government website, the Niwot Election Commission, Boulder County, or an incorporation campaign, and no public body collects anything through it.

Requests and questions reach the editor through the submission form, choosing “Something else”. That form is the editorial contact; there is no separate address to write to.

What is collected, and why

The submission form. When you send a listing, an event or a correction, we receive what you type: the kind of submission, the name of the business, event or page, the details, a link to your page or source, and your email address if you choose to give one. The email address is optional and is used only to reply about that submission.

Email notices. There is no notices form on the site and no mailing list is kept. Nothing here collects an address for that purpose. If notices are offered later, this page will say so, and will name whatever service delivers them, before anything is collected.

Server logs. The hosting provider records each request to the site — the address requested, the time, your IP address and browser details — for security and to keep the site running. We do not use these logs to identify readers.

Type from Google Fonts. The pages load two typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests the font files from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address and browser details under Google’s own privacy policy. Nothing else on the site is loaded from a third party.

Where it is processed and stored

Three services process anything at all, and they are the only three. Formspree receives what you type into the submission form and forwards it as an email to the editor’s mailbox; it keeps a copy in its submission archive so a lost email can be recovered, and is given the information for no other purpose. Vercel hosts the site and writes the server logs described above. Google Fonts serves the two typefaces, and your browser’s request for them reaches Google. All three operate in the United States. Nothing you send is sold or shared with advertisers, and nothing is passed to any organization beyond those three and the editor who reads it.

How long it is kept

A submission is kept for as long as it is needed to act on it. Where it leads to a published correction or listing, the submission is kept as the record of what changed and why, because corrections here are published with their date and source. A submission that leads nowhere is deleted within twelve months.

Email from this guide

No newsletter or notice list is operated, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from. The only email this guide sends is a reply to a submission, to the address given on that submission, about that submission.

Access and deletion

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Send the request through the submission form, choosing “Something else”, or reply to any notice. We answer within thirty days.

Cookies, analytics and tracking

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, carries no advertising and includes no third-party tracking scripts. The only requests that leave the site are the font files described above.

Questions and changes

Privacy questions go to the editor by the routes above. Changes to this page are published with the date they took effect and a note of what changed.
